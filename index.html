📓 简介Introduction

这是一个以原版生存为主要玩法的边界生存地图系列。你将从狭隘的空间启程，以智慧觅取资源，以勇气宣战昼夜，以经验开拓世界边界。

This is a world series built around vanilla-style border survival. You start in a cramped space, gather what you need with your wits, declare war on the night with courage, and push the World Border outward with experience. It is made for players who already know how vanilla plays.

🔆 核心特性Core Features

📕 世界边界World Border

世界边界是Minecraft1.8版本加入的原版特性，可前往MinecraftWiki-世界边界查阅详细信息。

The World Border is a vanilla feature that arrived in Minecraft 1.8. The Minecraft Wiki – World Border page has the details.

🥬 边界能量Border Energy

世界边界的大小，每级能量=0.5格边界范围，可通过边界升级功能牌消耗经验等级并提升边界能量，从而扩大世界边界。

Every level of Border Energy adds 0.5 blocks to the World Border's radius. Spend experience levels at the Border Upgrade sign to level it up and push the World Border outward.

📋️ 功能牌Function Signs

进入该系列的专属世界后，在世界出生点会看到三种功能告示牌，对其右键可触发对应功能。

  • 边界升级：消耗经验等级并提升边界能量。
  • 切换模式：在昼夜模式与禁锢模式之间切换。关于边界模式的更多信息，详见扩展特性。
  • 召唤神匠：在玩家当前位置生成神匠，每个游戏日只能召唤一次，已加载区块内最多存在一只。关于神匠的更多信息，详见扩展特性。
功能牌的硬度为-1，爆炸抗性为3,600,000。

Every world in this series puts three function signs at the world spawn point. Walk up, right-click one, and its function fires.

  • Border Upgrade — Spends experience levels to raise Border Energy.
  • Switch Mode — Flips between Day/Night Mode and Imprisoned Mode. See Expansion Features for what border modes actually do.
  • Summon Chaos Smith — Calls a Chaos Smith to the player's position, only once per game day. Only one can be alive in loaded chunks at a time. See Expansion Features for more on the Chaos Smith.
Function signs have a hardness of -1 and a blast resistance of 3,600,000.

🌙 扩展特性Expansion Features

💠 边界模式Border Modes

  • 含有禁锢、昼夜两种模式，可在游戏中进行切换。
  • Two modes: Imprisoned Mode and Day/Night Mode. The two can be switched between in game.

🔵 禁锢模式Imprisoned Mode

  • 常规默认模式。
  • The default mode.

🌗 昼夜模式Day/Night Mode

  • 昼夜模式的白天与禁锢模式并无区别。
  • 在昼夜模式的夜晚，刷怪上限将会提高至140、单次刷怪数量将会提高至10、怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至30%，且怪物将无视光照等级生成。
  • 在昼夜模式的夜晚，若边界能量至少拥有50级，将会额外扩展一倍空间。
  • During the day in Day/Night Mode, everything behaves exactly as it does in Imprisoned Mode.
  • At night in Day/Night Mode, the monster cap rises to 140, monsters per spawn rises to 10, the chance that monsters spawn wearing armor rises to 30%, and monsters spawn regardless of light level.
  • At night in Day/Night Mode, if Border Energy is at level 50 or higher, the space inside the World Border doubles.

🌙 昼夜模式 - 永恒之夜Day/Night Mode - Eternal Night

  • 在昼夜模式夜晚结束的一分钟前，若边界能量至少拥有100级，便会降临永恒之夜。
  • 永恒之夜降临后，世界出生点周围将会出现异界的永夜骑士。
  • 受永夜骑士影响，时间会被禁锢在他们降临的那一刻，世界将陷入永恒的雷暴之中。
  • 只有将所有降临的永夜骑士全部击败，才可解除其对世界的影响。
  • 之后，在度过或通过睡觉跳过0-4个昼夜模式的夜晚后，永恒之夜将会再次降临。
处于和平模式时，将立即结束永恒之夜，且不会尝试进入永恒之夜。
  • One minute before a night in Day/Night Mode ends, an Eternal Night descends if Border Energy has reached level 100.
  • Once the Eternal Night descends, Evernight Knights from another realm appear around the world spawn point.
  • Under their influence, time freezes at their arrival and the world sinks into an endless thunderstorm.
  • Their grip on the world breaks only when all the descending Evernight Knights are defeated.
  • The Eternal Night descends again after surviving or sleeping through 0–4 more nights in Day/Night Mode.
In Peaceful Mode an active Eternal Night ends immediately, and no new one will ever begin.

🧊 永恒雨夜 / 永恒雪夜Eternal Rain Night / Eternal Snow Night

  • 永恒之夜降临后，受永夜骑士的影响，边界可能会染上 苍白、凶红、夕橙、灿金、邃蓝、黯紫 六种色彩。
  • 根据影响程度的不同，世界气候将会发生紊乱，怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高。
  • 当永恒之夜状态下的边界处于灿金、夕橙、邃蓝状态时：所有生物群系都将异常降雨，无论寒冷或是温暖。怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至40%/50%/60%。
  • 当永恒之夜状态下的边界处于黯紫、凶红、苍白状态时：所有生物群系都将异常降雪，无论温暖或是寒冷。怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至70%/80%/90%。
  • 当永夜骑士被全部击败后，世界气候将会恢复正常，边界色彩将会逐渐恢复，并会以色彩预示下次永恒之夜的到来。
通过观察昼夜模式时间栏中的月相变化，也可预示下次永恒之夜的降临时刻。
  • Once the Eternal Night descends, under their influence the World Border can be stained with any of six colors: Pale White, Danger Red, Sun Orange, Bright Gold, Deep Blue, Dark Purple.
  • Their influence disrupts the climate, and the stronger it is, the greater the chance that monsters spawn wearing armor.
  • When the World Border shows Bright Gold, Sun Orange, or Deep Blue: every biome rains no matter whether it is cold or warm, and the armor chance rises to 40%/50%/60%.
  • When the World Border shows Dark Purple, Danger Red, or Pale White: every biome snows no matter whether it is warm or cold, and the armor chance rises to 70%/80%/90%.
  • Once every Evernight Knight is defeated, the climate recovers, the World Border's colors fade back, and the color signals when the next Eternal Night will arrive.
The arrival of the next Eternal Night can also be read off the moon phase on the Day/Night clock.
