📓 简介Introduction

这是一个以原版生存为主要玩法的边界生存地图系列。你将从狭隘的空间启程，以智慧觅取资源，以勇气宣战昼夜，以经验开拓世界边界。

This is a map series built around vanilla survival, played inside the World Border. You start boxed into a tiny world, gather what you need with your wits, take on the turning of day and night with courage, and push the border outward with experience. It is made for players who already know how vanilla plays.

🔆 核心特性Core Features

📕 世界边界World Border

世界边界是Minecraft1.8版本加入的原版特性，可前往MinecraftWiki-世界边界查阅详细信息。

The World Border is a vanilla feature that arrived in Minecraft 1.8. The Minecraft Wiki – World Border page covers the details.

🥬 边界能量Border Energy

世界边界的大小，每级能量=0.5格边界范围，可通过边界升级功能牌消耗经验等级并提升边界能量，从而扩大世界边界。

Every level of Border Energy adds 0.5 blocks to the border radius. Spend experience levels at the Border Upgrade sign to raise your Border Energy and push the border outward.

📋️ 功能牌Function Signs

进入该系列的专属世界后，在世界出生点会看到三种功能告示牌，对其右键可触发对应功能。

  • 边界升级：消耗经验等级并提升边界能量。
  • 切换模式：在昼夜模式与禁锢模式之间切换。关于边界模式的更多信息，详见扩展特性。
  • 召唤神匠：在玩家当前位置生成神匠，每个游戏日只能召唤一次，已加载区块内最多存在一只。关于神匠的更多信息，详见扩展特性。
功能牌的硬度为-1，爆炸抗性为3,600,000。

Every world in this series puts three function signs at the world spawn. Walk up, right-click one, and it fires.

  • Border Upgrade — Spends experience levels to raise Border Energy.
  • Switch Mode — Flips between Day/Night Mode and Locked Mode. See Expansion Features for what border modes actually do.
  • Summon Chaos Smith — Calls a Chaos Smith to your position, once per game day, with at most one alive in loaded chunks. See Expansion Features for more on the Chaos Smith.
Function signs have a hardness of -1 and a blast resistance of 3,600,000.

🌙 扩展特性Expansion Features

💠 边界模式Border Modes

  • 含有禁锢、昼夜两种模式，可在游戏中进行切换。
  • Two modes, Locked and Day/Night, and you can switch between them in game.

🔵 禁锢模式Locked Mode

  • 常规默认模式。
  • The default mode.

🌗 昼夜模式Day/Night Mode

  • 昼夜模式的白天与禁锢模式并无区别。
  • 在昼夜模式的夜晚，刷怪上限将会提高至140、单次刷怪数量将会提高至10、怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至30%，且怪物将无视光照等级生成。
  • 在昼夜模式的夜晚，若边界能量至少拥有50级，将会额外扩展一倍空间。
  • Daytime behaves exactly as it does in Locked Mode.
  • At night, the mob cap rises to 140, mobs per spawn rises to 10, the chance that mobs spawn wearing armor rises to 30%, and mobs spawn regardless of light level.
  • At night, Border Energy of level 50 or more doubles the space you have.

🌙 昼夜模式 - 永恒之夜Day/Night Mode - Eternal Night

  • 在昼夜模式夜晚结束的一分钟前，若边界能量至少拥有100级，便会降临永恒之夜。
  • 永恒之夜降临后，世界出生点周围将会出现异界的永夜骑士。
  • 受永夜骑士影响，时间会被禁锢在他们降临的那一刻，世界将陷入永恒的雷暴之中。
  • 只有将所有降临的永夜骑士全部击败，才可解除其对世界的影响。
  • 之后，在度过或通过睡觉跳过0-4个昼夜模式的夜晚后，永恒之夜将会再次降临。
处于和平模式时，将立即结束永恒之夜，且不会尝试进入永恒之夜。
  • One minute before a Day/Night night ends, an Eternal Night falls if your Border Energy has reached level 100.
  • Once it falls, Evernight Knights from another realm appear around the world spawn.
  • Under their influence, time locks to the moment they arrived and the world sinks into an endless thunderstorm.
  • Only defeating every Evernight Knight that descended breaks their hold on the world.
  • It returns once you have lived through or slept through 0–4 more Day/Night nights.
In Peaceful Mode an Eternal Night ends immediately, and none will ever begin.

🧊 永恒雨夜 / 永恒雪夜Eternal Rain Night / Eternal Snow Night

  • 永恒之夜降临后，受永夜骑士的影响，边界可能会染上 苍白、凶红、夕橙、灿金、邃蓝、黯紫 六种色彩。
  • 根据影响程度的不同，世界气候将会发生紊乱，怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高。
  • 当永恒之夜状态下的边界处于灿金、夕橙、邃蓝状态时：所有生物群系都将异常降雨，无论寒冷或是温暖。怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至40%/50%/60%。
  • 当永恒之夜状态下的边界处于黯紫、凶红、苍白状态时：所有生物群系都将异常降雪，无论温暖或是寒冷。怪物穿戴盔甲的概率将会提高至70%/80%/90%。
  • 当永夜骑士被全部击败后，世界气候将会恢复正常，边界色彩将会逐渐恢复，并会以色彩预示下次永恒之夜的到来。
通过观察昼夜模式时间栏中的月相变化，也可预示下次永恒之夜的降临时刻。
  • Once the Eternal Night falls, the Evernight Knights can stain the border with any of six colors: Pale White, Danger Red, Sun Orange, Bright Gold, Deep Blue, Dark Purple.
  • The stronger their influence, the more the climate unravels and the more likely mobs are to spawn wearing armor.
  • While the border shows Bright Gold, Sun Orange, or Deep Blue: every biome rains no matter whether it is cold or warm, and the armor chance rises to 40% / 50% / 60%.
  • While the border shows Dark Purple, Danger Red, or Pale White: every biome snows no matter whether it is warm or cold, and the armor chance rises to 70% / 80% / 90%.
  • Once every Evernight Knight is defeated, the climate recovers, the border colors fade back, and the color that returns warns you which kind of Eternal Night is coming next.
You can also read the arrival of the next Eternal Night off the moon phase on the Day/Night clock.

🐎 永夜骑士Evernight Knight

  • 永夜骑士的生命值上限会随着边界能量的提高而提高。
  • 当永夜骑士周围不存在玩家时，其将持续恢复生命值。
  • 当永夜骑士受到伤害时，将会干扰世界生成更多怪物。
  • 永夜骑士被玩家击败后，会掉落一个随机维度宝藏箱。
  • 同时，因其为世界之虫(bug)，所有怪物都欲图消灭它。
永夜骑士仅会出现在以世界出生点为中心，半径16格正方形范围内的随机位置的最高露天处，洞穴型世界出现的位置最高不超过Y96。
永夜骑士极为危险，建议在具有足够的防御与治疗手段后再尝试与其进行武力决斗。
永夜骑士不会离开当前维度，不会乘坐载具。
  • An Evernight Knight's max health climbs along with your Border Energy.
  • It regenerates health for as long as no player is nearby.
  • Every time it takes damage, the world itself stirs and spawns more mobs.
  • Defeating one makes it drop a random dimension's loot chest.
  • Because it counts as a World Bug, every mob wants it dead.
Evernight Knights only appear at random spots inside a 16-block square centered on the world spawn, always on the highest exposed ground; in cave-type worlds they never appear above Y 96.
Evernight Knights are extremely dangerous. Bring solid defenses and real healing before you try to duel one.
Evernight Knights never leave their dimension and never ride vehicles.

  • Build a portal frame out of a dimension's own theme blocks and light it with a fire source to travel to that dimension.

  • Every Persisting dimension keeps its own Border Energy, except the Nether and the End.
  • Every Persisting dimension has its own Sustainer, except the Nether and the End.
  • Evernight Knights only descend into the Overworld or a Persisting dimension whose Border Energy has reached level 100, excluding the Nether and the End.
  • If several dimensions hold level 100 or more, Evernight Knights descend at random into one of them that has at least one player who is not in Spectator Mode.
  • If no dimension qualifies, they try the Overworld first; if its Border Energy is below level 100, they do not descend at all.

  3. Extract the world pack into the server root and rename the world save to world, or to whatever level-name is set to in server.properties.
  4. Delete the mcwifipnp mod (*Advanced Multiplayer Settings*).
  5. Copy config/carpet/default_carpet.conf into the world save folder (usually world) and rename it carpet.conf to enable the carpet mod features.
  6. Once the setup is done, start the server.
